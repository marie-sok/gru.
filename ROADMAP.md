# gru. roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not a release promise. Priorities can move as the product is tested.

## Now — make the core dependable

- harden authentication and expired-session handling;
- keep WebSocket reconnects predictable and recoverable;
- finish message state consistency across REST and realtime updates;
- stabilize audio recording and playback;
- refine short video notes and media persistence;
- remove UI noise and keep the chat surface lightweight.

## Next — make it feel complete

- push notifications with APNs;
- profile and avatar editing;
- richer contact discovery;
- ordinary video and document attachments;
- polished media preview and retry states;
- theme system with a recognisable GRU visual identity;
- onboarding and first-run experience.

## Then — prepare for real users

- TestFlight distribution;
- crash and performance monitoring;
- privacy review and production security pass;
- backend deployment hardening;
- rate limits and abuse protections;
- backup/recovery strategy;
- product analytics limited to what is actually useful and respectful.

## Long-term direction

The ambition is not to become a feature checklist. The ambition is to make `gru.` feel unusually coherent: a messenger with its own visual language, thoughtful realtime behaviour and enough restraint to stay personal.
